The Story

A Long Way
From Home

Three young aliens — Zib, Mara, and Pip — were just trying to take a shortcut through the Milky Way when their ship hit an unexpected asteroid shower.

They crash-land in the backyard of Maya, a curious girl who spends her days in a treehouse full of maps, books, and half-finished inventions. She has never met an alien before. Then again, she has never had a real friend before either.

Together, they will have to navigate the strangest planet in the galaxy — Earth — and figure out how to get the little ship back to the stars.

Friendship & Wonder

The Treehouse
at the Edge
of Everything

Maya's treehouse becomes their home base — a place where four very different beings discover they have more in common than they ever expected.

Zib is fascinated by water (it does not exist on their planet). Mara learns to love music — sound, she says, is like colour for ears. And tiny Pip, the youngest, just wants to stay forever.

Through star maps and mud pies, through laughter and a few tears, these four friends will show each other what it means to truly belong somewhere.

The Illustrator

Meet
Konstantina

Konstantina is a children's book illustrator with a love for whimsical worlds, unexpected friendships, and stories that speak softly to both children and the adults who read alongside them.

Her signature watercolour-on-paper style brings a warm, handmade quality to every page — textures you almost want to touch, colours that feel like a memory of something beautiful.

Lost Among Stars is her debut picture book, born from a childhood fascination with space and the question: what if someone very different showed up and needed your help?
